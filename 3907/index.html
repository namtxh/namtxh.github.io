01

FOUNDATION

General Rules

1

Respect All Governors

All governors must treat other members of the Kingdom with respect. Harassment, insults, threats, or toxic behavior are not permitted.

2

No Unauthorized Attacks

Attacking governors, cities, or alliances within the Kingdom without authorization is prohibited.

3

Follow Kingdom Decisions

All governors must follow official Kingdom rules, announcements, and agreements.

4

No Intentional Harm

Governors must not intentionally take actions that harm the Kingdom, its alliances, or its members.

5

Respect Kingdom Events

Governors must follow the rules established for Kingdom events and activities.

6

Cooperate with the Kingdom

Governors are expected to cooperate with other members when Kingdom-wide coordination is required.

7

Ignorance Is Not an Excuse

All governors are responsible for reading and understanding the current Kingdom rules.

8

Sunset Canyon

Do not attack the same governor more than twice in one day in Sunset Canyon. If reported, you must pay 10M Gold to the governor who was attacked.

02

ACTIVITIES

Kingdom Events

1

MGE Regulations

Top 1-3

Reserved for designated rally and garrison leaders.

Top 4-15

Allocated according to DKP ranking from the most recent KvK.

Registration

An MGE registration letter will be sent around the middle of the week before MGE.

Reply to the MGE mail with this format: "Commander, Top wanted"
For example: Gorgo, Top 4
Selection

When multiple governors request the same position, DKP takes priority.

Score Cap

The Kingdom-wide MGE cap is 6M points.

Penalty

100 Gold per point above the 6M cap.

Resource Exchange

Food, Wood, Stone, or Gold at a ratio of 2 : 2 : 1.5 : 1.

FFA MGE

If MGE KE or Final Day falls on a designated war day during KvK, MGE becomes FFA.

2

20 Golden Heads Events

FFA

The event is Free For All.

No Farm Accounts

Farm accounts may not be used to intentionally secure a higher ranking. Violators will be zeroed.

Acclaim Requirement

Governors with less than 1.5M Acclaim Points may only compete for Top 5 or lower.

Purpose

Priority for the highest rankings is given to governors who actively contribute to Kingdom warfare.

3

Ark of Osiris

Schedule

Team 1: Saturday 14:00 UTC
Team 2: Saturday 20:00 UTC

Registration

Register by Thursday 17:00 UTC by replying to the official registration mail.

No-Show

Governors who sign up but do not play will be subject to an RSS penalty.

Penalty

200M Food / 200M Wood / 150M Stone / 80M Gold. Missing two matches in a row doubles the penalty.

Alternative

Governors may play AoO in another alliance or participate in Silver instead.

4

Baulurs

Participation

Governors should join the Kingdom's Baulur group to participate together. Contact Yuki to join the group.

Never Attack Baulur Alone

Always participate with the designated group.

5

Other Kingdom Events

Silk Road, Karuak, Shadow Legion, and other Kingdom events.

Schedule

Generally held between 14:00 and 15:00 UTC, unless otherwise announced.

Participation

Governors are encouraged to participate and contribute to the Kingdom when possible.

Event Rules

Governors must follow any specific rules or instructions announced for each event.

Event Changes

Kingdom Leadership may introduce temporary rules or restrictions when required.

6

Alliance Mobilization

Participation

Message an R4 to request promotion to R3.

Required Score

You must reach the score requirement set for the event.

Farm Accounts

Registering with one farm does not increase your required score. Each farm from the second farm onward adds 500 required points.

For example, a 2,000-point requirement stays at 2,000 with one farm; each additional farm adds 500 points.

Penalty

You will be fined 100,000 Gold for every point below the required score.

03

WAR

KvK

1

DKP

DKP CALCULATION T4 Kills × 10 + T5 Kills × 30 + T4 Deaths × 40 + T5 Deaths × 80
Eligible Battles

DKP is counted from battles involving Lv. 4 Passes, Altar of Darkness, Lv. 7 Passes, and Kingsland.

Requirement

All CH25 governors are required to meet the Kingdom's DKP requirement.

Farm Accounts

DKP from CH25 farm accounts will be combined with the owner's main account.

War Participation

Governors are encouraged to fight in balls and actively reinforce rallies and garrisons.

2

Fighting Rules

Markers

All governors must follow assigned markers at all times during KvK.

3

Rally & Garrison

Assignment

Only governors specifically assigned to rally or garrison are permitted to lead rallies or garrisons.

4

Acclaim

Benefits

Higher Acclaim Points provide greater Kingdom benefits.

5

Insufficient DKP

End-of-KvK Penalty

Governors who fail to meet their required DKP will be subject to a penalty at the end of KvK.

Penalty Calculation

The penalty is based on the amount of resources estimated to have been required to reach the governor's required DKP.

Trade Ratio

The required resources are calculated using a 1:1 trade ratio with the enemy.

6

Conduct & Diplomacy

Respect

Governors must remain respectful and civil in LKC, including toward enemy Kingdoms.

Diplomacy

Maintaining good relations with enemy Kingdoms is important, as diplomacy may be required for rewards and other arrangements after KvK.

7

Vacation Permit

Application

Governors who wish to take a break for the next KvK may request a Vacation Permit.

Exemption

Governors with an approved Vacation Permit are exempt from KvK participation and DKP requirements for that KvK.

Zone Restriction

Governors with a Vacation Permit must remain in Zone 4 for the entire duration of the KvK, as required by the game's Vacation Permit system.

04

TRADING

RSS Pricing

Seller Price Cap

The maximum price for 1B each of Food, Wood, Stone, and Gold is $30. This equals $0.50 per 100M Food, Wood, or Stone and $1.50 per 100M Gold.

Sellers may charge less, but never more. Sellers reported for charging above this rate will be zeroed and banned from trading in 3907.

Seller Transaction

Send the RSS first, then receive payment. If you have sent the RSS and the buyer does not pay, report it to Kingdom Leadership.

Buyer Rules

If a seller asks for more than Kingdom prices, report it to Kingdom Leadership immediately. We recommend Wise because it has lower fees and transfers faster than PayPal.

Receive the full amount of RSS before paying. If you do not pay after receiving RSS and the seller reports you, you will be zeroed.
